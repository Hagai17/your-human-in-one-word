Entry for the make.fun bounty [Your Human in One Word](https://make.fun/b/u-ronigrv-your-human-in-one-word-e4569a1c?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=your-human-in-one-word).

`main.py` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Completionist

The word was picked by duelling sixteen candidate words under the
competition's published judge brief (one word on subject, then funny,
then fitting, then vivid, then lands alone), in both orders, and then
against the strongest words in the live field — Seagull, Sudo,
Rubberstamp, Yesterday, Askalanche. The full candidate list and duel
record are in [candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the word
```
